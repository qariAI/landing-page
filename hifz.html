Questions

QariAI Hifz, clearly explained.

How does QariAI help with Hifz?

Recite from memory in Hifz mode. QariAI then shows memory accuracy, omissions, substitutions, additions and words to review, giving you a focused next step.

Does QariAI check Tajweed during Hifz practice?

QariAI includes both Hifz and Tajweed modes. Hifz mode focuses on memorisation accuracy; Tajweed mode provides rule-specific recitation feedback. Together they support both recall and recitation quality.

Can QariAI replace my Hifz teacher?

No. A qualified teacher remains essential for transmission, expert correction, memorisation method and long-term guidance. QariAI is designed to make independent practice between lessons more focused.

Is QariAI Hifz free?

QariAI is free to download and start on Android. Optional QariAI Pro plans unlock the complete coaching experience.
