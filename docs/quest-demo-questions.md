# Qur'an Quest "Sample questions" demo

**STATUS: NEEDS REVIEW before merge.** A teacher or scholar must verify every question, answer and citation below, and the Arabic text in particular, before this goes to production.

Where: the `#quest` section of `index.html`, `ar.html`, `fr.html`, `ms.html` (plain inline JS, no network, no storage). It is labelled "Sample questions" and says it is a short sample, not the game itself. It does not show maze mechanics.

Citation format shown to the reader: `Citation: <Surah name>, <surah>:<verse>`.

| # | Question (English) | Options (correct in bold) | Citation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 **(NEW, replaces the Al-Ikhlas question)** | In Surah Al-Fatihah, we ask Allah to guide us to the straight… | Door / **Path** / Garden | Al-Fatihah, 1:6 |
| 2 | Surah Ash-Sharh says that with hardship comes… | Wealth / Fear / **Ease** | Ash-Sharh, 94:6 (the same statement is also in 94:5) |
| 3 | In which surah is Ayat al-Kursi, the Throne Verse? | Al-Fatihah / **Al-Baqarah** / Yasin | Al-Baqarah, 2:255 |

Question 1 is numbered with the Hafs convention (Al-Fatihah 1:1 is the Basmalah, so "guide us to the straight path" is 1:6). Please confirm. Question 3's third option was changed from Al-Ikhlas to Yasin so that Al-Ikhlas no longer appears anywhere in the demo.

Review notes for the teacher:
- The questions paraphrase the meaning. No full Qur'anic verse is quoted in Arabic. The new question 1 uses the paraphrase "طريق مستقيم" (straight way) as the answer rather than quoting the verse; please confirm or reword. The only bare Qur'anic word is يسرًا (option for question 2).
- Citations use Western digits and a comma after the surah name; confirm the preferred Arabic convention, the surah-name spellings (الفاتحة، الشرح، البقرة، يس) and the "سورة" prefix.
- Confirm the surah names used in each language (Ash-Sharh is also called Al-Inshirah).
