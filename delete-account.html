Account & Data

Delete Your QariAI Account

If you signed in to QariAI with Google, this page explains how to request deletion of your account and the data tied to it.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Email us at salam@qariai.app from the email address linked to your QariAI account (the same address you used to sign in with Google).
  2. Use the subject line "Account Deletion Request" so it's routed quickly.
  3. Include your account email in the body of the message, so we can locate and remove the correct account.

Requests are processed within 30 days. You'll receive a confirmation once deletion is complete.

What Gets Deleted

Deleted
  • Your account record (name, photo, email from Google Sign-In)
  • Recitation progress, stats, and achievements
  • Any saved Hifz revision history tied to your account
Not affected
  • Guest/local progress stored only on your device
  • Purchase records retained as required for billing and legal purposes

Using QariAI Without an Account

QariAI works without signing in — recitations and progress in guest mode are stored only on your device, in your browser or app storage. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling QariAI, without needing to contact us.

Ready to request deletion?

Email us with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and we'll take care of the rest.
