Questions

Who can partner with QariAI?

Islamic content creators, podcasters and newsletter writers, mosque and halaqah organisers, madrasahs, and independent Qur’an teachers who want to encourage consistent daily recitation practice.

How does the revenue share work?

Approved creators get a personal referral link and promo code. You earn a 25% recurring revenue share on Pro subscriptions that come through your link or code. We will confirm the exact terms with you when we approve your application.

Are the mosque and madrasah pilots free?

Yes. Mosques, study circles and madrasahs get free early access to a private group pilot for their community. We set it up with you after you apply.

Does QariAI replace a teacher?

No. QariAI is built to support qualified teachers, not replace them. It helps students practise between lessons so live class time can go deeper.
