Iqlab in Tajweed: Turning Noon into Meem
Letter, Rule and Examples

Iqlab (إقلاب) is the turning over of a noon sakinah or tanween into a hidden meem before one letter only: ب.

What is iqlab?

ب

Iqlab means "turning over" or "changing". When a noon sakinah (نْ) or tanween is followed by ب, you do not say the noon. Its sound is changed into a meem, hidden with a ghunnah, and then you release into the ب.

Say مِن بَعْدِ slowly. You do not hear a noon. You hear a light, hidden meem with a nasal hum, then the ب. That is iqlab.

How iqlab sounds

✅
Lips lightly closed

The lips touch gently for the hidden meem. Do not press them hard.

✅
Ghunnah for two counts

Hold the nasal sound steadily, about two counts, then release into the ب.

💡
A quick check

If your tongue touches the roof of the mouth, you said a noon. The tongue stays down and the lips do the work.

Iqlab in the four noon sakinah rules

Izhar6 throat lettersPronounced clearly

Examples from the Quran

Across two words: مِن بَعْدِ (2:27), مِن بَيْنِ يَدَيْهِ (41:42). With tanween: سَمِيعٌ بَصِيرٌ (22:61) and عَلِيمٌ بِذَاتِ (3:119). Inside one word: أَنبِئْهُم (2:33) and لَيُنبَذَنَّ (104:4).

Common mistakes

❌
Pronouncing a clear noon

Saying نْ before ب. The noon must turn into a meem sound.

❌
Pressing the lips hard

The meem is hidden, so the lips close lightly.

❌
Dropping the ghunnah

Turning it into a plain meem with no held nasal sound.

❌
Skipping iqlab inside a word

Treating لَيُنبَذَنَّ or أَنبِئْهُم as clear. Iqlab applies inside one word too.

A simple iqlab practice

Step 1: Say مِن بَعْدِ slowly, lips lightly closed, ghunnah for two counts. Step 2: Say سَمِيعٌ بَصِيرٌ and عَلِيمٌ بِذَاتِ. Step 3: Say أَنبِئْهُم. Record yourself and compare with a teacher or with QariAI.

Iqlab FAQ

What is iqlab in tajweed?

Iqlab (إقلاب) means turning over. When a noon sakinah or tanween is followed by the letter ب, the noon sound is changed into a meem, hidden with a ghunnah held for about two counts.

Which letter causes iqlab?

Only one letter: ب (baa). That makes iqlab the simplest of the four noon sakinah rules to recognise.

How do I pronounce iqlab?

Say a hidden meem instead of the noon: close the lips lightly, without pressing them, hold the ghunnah for about two counts, then release into the ب. For example مِن بَعْدِ sounds like مِمْ بَعْدِ.

Does iqlab apply inside one word?

Yes. In لَيُنبَذَنَّ (104:4) and أَنبِئْهُم (2:33) the noon and the ب are in the same word, and iqlab still applies.

How is iqlab marked in the mushaf?

Most printed mushafs write a small meem above the noon or tanween, or a small meem in place of the tanween's second mark, to show that the sound becomes a meem.

What is the difference between iqlab and ikhfa shafawi?

Both use a hidden meem sound with the lips lightly closed. Iqlab starts from a noon sakinah or tanween before ب. Ikhfa shafawi starts from a meem sakinah before ب.

How do I practise iqlab?

Say مِن بَعْدِ، سَمِيعٌ بَصِيرٌ، أَنبِئْهُم slowly. Close the lips lightly, hold the nasal sound for two counts, then open into the ب. Record yourself and compare with a teacher or with QariAI.

Iqlab quick reference

  • Trigger: noon sakinah or tanween.
  • Letter: only ب.
  • Sound: hidden meem with about two counts of ghunnah.
  • Where: inside one word or across two words.
