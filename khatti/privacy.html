Privacy

Khatti - Arabic Calligraphy · last updated 7 November 2026

In short: Khatti has no accounts and the app collects no personal data. Your practice stays on your device.

No accounts

You never sign up or sign in. Khatti does not ask for your name, email, phone number or any other personal details.

Your progress stays on your device

Your Path progress, stars, drawings and settings are saved only in your browser’s or app’s local storage on this device. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Clearing the app’s or browser’s data removes them.

Backups stay with you

“Back up / restore” creates a file that is saved to your own device. We never receive it. You decide where to keep it and whether to share it.

The only external request

Khatti loads Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookieless, privacy-first page counter. It records anonymous, aggregate visit counts (such as page views and country). It sets no cookies, uses no identifiers to track you across sites, and does not collect personal data. No other analytics, advertising or third-party trackers are used.

No ads, no permissions

Khatti shows no ads and does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files (beyond a file you choose yourself when restoring a backup).

Children

Khatti is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
