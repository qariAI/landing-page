Madd in Tajweed: Prolongation
Types, Counts and Examples

Madd (مَدّ) is the prolongation of the sounds ا و ي. The base length is two counts, and the other types stretch it to four, five or six.

What is madd?

ـَا
ـُو
ـِي

Madd means "extension". It applies to three letters when they carry a long vowel sound: alif after a fatha, waw sakinah after a damma, and ya sakinah after a kasra. A count (harakah) is roughly the time it takes to open or close a finger at a steady pace.

Say قَالَ, قِيلَ, يَقُولُ. The vowel in each is a long one. Holding it for two counts is the natural madd, and every other type is measured from it.

The six main types of madd

This table gives each type, what triggers it, how long to hold it and a Quran example (Hafs from Asim):

TypeTriggerCountsExample
Madd asli (natural)A madd letter with no hamza or sukoon after it2قَالَ · يَقُولُ · قِيلَ
Madd muttasil (connected)Madd letter, then hamza in the same word. Obligatory.4 or 5جَاءَ · السَّمَاءِ
Madd munfasil (separated)Madd letter ends a word, hamza starts the next. Permissible.4 or 5يَا أَيُّهَا
Madd lazim (necessary)Madd letter, then a permanent sukoon or shaddah in the same word6الضَّالِّينَ · الْحَاقَّةُ
Madd arid lis-sukunMadd letter, then a letter that becomes sakin only when you stop2, 4 or 6الرَّحِيمِ · نَسْتَعِينُ
Madd liinSakin waw or ya after a fatha, when you stop2, 4 or 6خَوْفٍ · قُرَيْشٍ

Some schools allow different lengths for munfasil, and teachers sometimes name the minor types differently, so follow your own teacher's terms and lengths.

How madd should sound

✅
Steady length

Hold the sound at an even volume and pace for the full count, then release cleanly.

✅
Consistent counts

Pick one length for muttasil and munfasil and keep it the same throughout your recitation.

💡
A quick check

Tap a finger once per count while you recite. If the taps and the sound end together, the length is right.

Examples from the Quran

The word الضَّالِّينَ (1:7) at the end of Al-Fatihah is the classic madd lazim: the alif is followed by a shaddah, so it is held for six counts. In جَاءَ نَصْرُ اللَّهِ (110:1) the alif is followed by a hamza in the same word, which is madd muttasil.

In يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ (2:21) the alif ends one word and the hamza begins the next, which is madd munfasil. When you stop on الرَّحِيمِ (1:3) the madd is arid lis-sukun, and when you stop on قُرَيْشٍ (106:1) the ya after a fatha gives madd liin.

Common mistakes

❌
Cutting the madd short

The most common mistake. The long vowel is pronounced as a short one.

❌
Over-stretching the natural madd

Holding a two-count madd for four or more turns it into a different rule.

❌
Uneven counts

Reciting one muttasil for four counts and the next for six. Keep the length consistent.

❌
Madd on the wrong letter

Stretching a letter that is not a madd letter, such as a waw with a vowel on it.

A simple madd practice

Step 1: Recite قَالَ، قِيلَ، يَقُولُ for two counts each. Step 2: Recite جَاءَ and يَا أَيُّهَا for four or five counts, tapping a finger at a steady pace. Step 3: Apply it to Al-Fatihah, holding الضَّالِّينَ for six. Record yourself and compare with a teacher or with QariAI.

Madd FAQ

What is madd in tajweed?

Madd (مَدّ) means prolongation. In tajweed it is the lengthening of the sound of a madd letter: alif after a fatha, waw sakinah after a damma, or ya sakinah after a kasra.

What are the letters of madd?

Three letters: alif (ا) preceded by a fatha, waw (و) with sukoon preceded by a damma, and ya (ي) with sukoon preceded by a kasra. Teachers often remember them with the word نُوحِيهَا (و ي ا).

How many counts is a natural madd?

Two counts (harakat). The natural madd (madd asli or tabii) is the base length. Every other madd is measured against it, so shortening it or stretching it is a mistake.

What is the difference between madd muttasil and munfasil?

In madd muttasil (connected) the madd letter and the hamza are in the same word, as in جَاءَ, and the lengthening is obligatory. In madd munfasil (separated) the madd letter ends one word and the hamza begins the next, as in يَا أَيُّهَا, and the lengthening is permissible.

How many counts do muttasil and munfasil take?

In the recitation of Hafs from Asim, teachers most often give 4 or 5 counts for both. Some schools allow 2 to 5 for munfasil. Choose one length, keep it steady through the whole recitation and follow your teacher.

What is madd lazim?

The necessary madd. A madd letter is followed by a permanent sukoon or shaddah in the same word, as in الضَّالِّينَ and الْحَاقَّةُ. It is held for 6 counts.

What is madd arid lis-sukun?

A madd that appears only when you stop. When you pause on a word such as الرَّحِيمِ, the last letter takes a temporary sukoon after a madd letter, and you may hold it 2, 4 or 6 counts.

How do I practise madd?

Recite قَالَ، قِيلَ، يَقُولُ for two counts each, then جَاءَ and السَّمَاءِ for four or five. Tap a finger at a steady pace, record yourself and compare with a teacher or with QariAI.

Madd quick reference

  • Letters: alif after fatha, waw after damma, ya after kasra.
  • Base length: two counts (natural madd).
  • Longer types: muttasil and munfasil 4 or 5, lazim 6, arid and liin 2, 4 or 6.
  • Key rule: pick a length and keep it consistent.
