Our manifesto

From extractive screen time to intentional digital sanctuaries.

Most apps are designed to take attention. We design ours to give something back: a few minutes of Tajweed, a verse revised, a word of the Qur’an understood. The habit of picking up the phone isn’t going away, so we aim to make it beneficial and productive.

We would rather you close the app after ten focused minutes than lose an hour inside it.Qaf Studio

Extractive screen time

  • Infinite feeds
  • Streaks that guilt you
  • Noise, badges, alerts
  • Success = minutes spent

Intentional sanctuaries

  • Short, finished sessions
  • Progress you can feel
  • Calm, uncluttered screens
  • Success = what you learned

How we work

Built with care for the Book it serves.

Benefit over attention

We measure success by what users learn, not by how long they stay.

Respect for the text

The Qur’anic text is treated with care, and features are designed to support learning, not to replace it.

Teachers first

Our tools are for practice between lessons. They complement qualified teachers; they don’t replace them.
