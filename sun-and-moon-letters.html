Sun and Moon Letters
Lam Shamsiyah and Lam Qamariyah

The letters split into 14 sun letters, where the lam of ال is absorbed, and 14 moon letters, where it is pronounced.

What are sun and moon letters?

Every noun with ال ("the") starts with one of 28 letters. Half of them make the lam of ال disappear into the next letter, and half leave it clear. The names come from two examples: الشَّمْس (the sun) and الْقَمَر (the moon).

How to tell from the text

✅
Sun letter

The lam has no sukoon and the next letter has a shaddah, as in الشَّمْس.

✅
Moon letter

The lam has a sukoon and the next letter has none, as in الْقَمَر.

💡
A quick check

If the letter after ال is doubled, it is a sun letter. If you pronounce the lam, it is a moon letter.

Common mistakes

❌
Saying "al" before a sun letter

Pronouncing the lam in الشَّمْس or الصَّلَاة. The lam must be absorbed.

❌
Absorbing the lam before a moon letter

Saying "aq-qamar" for الْقَمَر. The lam must stay clear.

❌
Forgetting the doubling

Dropping the lam but not doubling the sun letter.

A simple practice

Step 1: Say pairs: الشَّمْس والْقَمَر, النَّاس والْكِتَاب, الصَّلَاة والْحَمْد. Step 2: In each pair, check the first has a doubled letter and the second a clear lam. Record yourself and compare with a teacher or with QariAI.

Sun and moon letters FAQ

What are sun letters and moon letters?

The Arabic letters are split into two groups of 14 by how they behave after ال (the definite article). With sun letters (حروف شمسية) the lam of ال is not pronounced and the next letter is doubled. With moon letters (حروف قمرية) the lam is pronounced clearly.

What are the 14 sun letters?

ت ث د ذ ر ز س ش ص ض ط ظ ل ن. Examples: الشَّمْس, النَّاس, الصَّلَاة, الرَّحْمَٰن.

What are the 14 moon letters?

ا ب ج ح خ ع غ ف ق ك م ه و ي. Examples: الْقَمَر, الْكِتَاب, الْحَمْد, الْعَالَمِين.

Why is it called Lam Shamsiyah?

The word الشَّمْس (the sun) begins with a sun letter, so the lam is absorbed. The word الْقَمَر (the moon) begins with a moon letter, so the lam is pronounced. The names come from these two examples.

How can I tell from the writing?

With a sun letter, the lam has no sukoon and the next letter carries a shaddah, as in الشَّمْس. With a moon letter, the lam carries a sukoon, as in الْقَمَر.

Is the lam ever silent in the middle of a sentence?

With sun letters the lam is never pronounced, whether you begin with ال or join it to the previous word. With moon letters it is always pronounced.

How do I practise?

Say الشَّمْس والْقَمَر, النَّاس والْكِتَاب, الصَّلَاة والْحَمْد in pairs, listening for the doubled letter in the first of each pair and the clear lam in the second. Record yourself and compare with a teacher or with QariAI.

Quick reference

  • Sun letters (14): ت ث د ذ ر ز س ش ص ض ط ظ ل ن, lam absorbed.
  • Moon letters (14): ا ب ج ح خ ع غ ف ق ك م ه و ي, lam pronounced.
